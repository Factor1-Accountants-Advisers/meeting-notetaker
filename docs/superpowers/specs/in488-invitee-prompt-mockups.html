Meeting Notetaker · IN-488 · design mockups · 15 Sep 2026

Ask before the transcript goes to invitees

When a meeting's notes are ready, nothing is emailed until the recorder answers one question. Just me and a timed-out prompt both send to the organiser only. Invitees can still be sent the transcript later from the app. The same question appears as a Windows toast and as a card in the app; answering in either place resolves both.

Windows toastIn-app card
1 · Windows toast

Notes are ready, the prompt appears

Fires the moment processing finishes, from the main process, whether or not the window is open. Silent toast; the app plays its usual chime.

  • Email invitees → one email to organiser + invitees, plus SharePoint view access for them.
  • Just me → one email to the organiser only.
  • No answer for 2 min → same as Just me. safe default
  • Clicking the body opens the app; the timer keeps running.

Windows allows three text lines, so the toast shows the first three names and a count. The full list is in the app card.

2 · In the app, at the same time

The same question as a card on Home

Post-capture state awaiting_invitees. Shown while the toast is up, so the user can answer wherever they are looking.

  • Full invitee list, already filtered to allowed company domains.
  • Answering here closes the toast and cancels its timer.
  • No Dismiss on this card: the timeout resolves it.
  • The top bar stays quiet; waiting for an answer is not "processing".

3 · After the answer

One delivery pass, two possible recipient lists

Either answer runs the existing SharePoint-then-email sequence once. The card moves through emailing to today's ready or email_failed.

  • Email invitees: the ready message lists every address that received it, as it does today.
  • Just me / timeout: organiser only, and the card gains a Send to 5 invitees action so the decision can be changed later.

4 · Send later

Changing "Just me" to "everyone"

Send to 5 invitees records the new answer and runs the same delivery pass again. The backend knows the organiser already has their copy, so only the invitees are emailed, and the SharePoint files are re-saved with view access added.

  • Success lists the addresses reached by this send.
  • Failure keeps the existing Retry email; retrying can never email the organiser twice.

5 · After a restart

Unanswered and unsent meetings come back as cards

On launch the app checks the last 7 days of meetings and surfaces two kinds, above the New meeting card, the same way unuploaded recordings do today.

  • Still waiting for an answer (the app closed during the hold): the question again, no timer, no toast.
  • Sent to you only: the Send to invitees action, until dismissed.
  • Dismiss is remembered on this computer, so a card does not nag every launch.

When the question is skipped

  • No eligible invitees. A recording with nobody else on the invite (or only external addresses, which the allowlist removes) delivers to the organiser straight away, as today.
  • Fleet override. If IT sets MN_DELIVERY_RECIPIENTS=attendees at release, every meeting is treated as "Email invitees" with no prompt: the existing escape hatch, unchanged.
  • Per-user "always send" setting. Not in this version. To be confirmed with the team (DA's ticket proposed it; the sync raised the risk that what was said matters more than the preference).
Names and addresses other than the signed-in user and David Ahlhaus are examples. Card colours, spacing, and type are the app's own tokens (index.css); the toast is drawn to Windows 11 proportions and will look like the app's other reminder toasts. Backend and desktop details are in the design spec that accompanies this file.
